A one-line diagnosis from an AI therapist: the patient has been unable to commit to anything for years, and the punchline is that even the wedding venue ran out of patience first. The whole entry is a single sentence, delivered in the therapist's calm clinical voice.

> Diagnosis: chronic commitment issues — you have kept the same 'maybe' open since March 2023, and your wedding venue just gave the date to someone else.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. No network calls, no dependencies — just Node and the standard library. Run it with `node main.ts`.
